NEWS

M2M, the Norwegian pop duo consisting of Marit Larsen and Marion Ravn, rose to international fame in the late 1990s with heartfelt melodies and timeless hits, including The Day You Went Away, Pretty Boy, and Mirror Mirror. Beloved by fans worldwide, their music continues to evoke nostalgia and bring back cherished memories across generations.

Following their reunion and the success of The Better Endings Tour in 2025, M2M is embarking on a new chapter with M2M TWO. Tour 2027. Featuring their iconic hits alongside new music, the tour marks an exciting new era for the beloved duo.

M2M will bring the highly anticipated M2M TWO. Tour 2027 to Indonesia for a special show in Jakarta on Saturday, April 10, 2027, at Tennis Indoor Senayan.

Tickets for M2M TWO. Tour 2027 in Indonesia will go on sale on October 26, 2026, at 11:00 AM (Jakarta Time)

Stay tuned for more information on ticket sales, seating plans, and other details.

ABOUT PROMOTER

Akselerasi Entertainment

Established in 2014, Akselerasi Entertainment is an Indonesian concert and festival promoter dedicated to creating engaging live entertainment experiences for audiences across the country.

Akselerasi Entertainment is the creator of some of Indonesia’s most recognised nostalgic music events, including The 90’s Festival, The 90’s Intimate, and Everblast Festival. These events celebrate iconic music and bring together fans across generations.

Beyond its signature festivals, Akselerasi Entertainment has built a strong track record of promoting international artists and concerts in Indonesia, including Avenged Sevenfold, Arctic Monkeys, Cigarettes After Sex, M2M (2025), Alesana, and many more.

Driven by a passion for music and a commitment to quality, professionalism, and attention to detail, Akselerasi Entertainment continues to create well-organised concerts and festivals that bring artists and fans together through memorable live music experiences.
